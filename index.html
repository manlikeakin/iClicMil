WHY iCLICMIL?

Building Africa's Next Generation of Critical Thinkers

Every day, millions of young Africans consume content on social media, messaging apps and AI platforms. Unfortunately, misinformation spreads faster than facts. iClicMIL was created to make Media and Information Literacy simple, engaging and accessible for everyone.

  • ✔ Learn from trusted sources
  • ✔ Play interactive truth games
  • ✔ Verify online claims instantly
  • ✔ Share verified information beautifully
